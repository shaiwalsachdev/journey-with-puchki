To My Forever Puchki • October 29, 2026

"Watching you keep this sacred fast for me... all I wanted was for tonight's moon to hurry up! 🌙💖"

October 29, 2026 will forever be etched in my heart as one of the most emotional, sacred, and deeply touching milestones of our journey — our very first Karwa Chauth. From the earliest morning hours, you took upon yourself the strict Nirjala fast for me, not touching a single grain of food or drop of water all day, carrying nothing but selfless love, quiet grace, and unwavering devotion.

When evening came, you were breathtaking. Dressed in your exquisite red embroidered bridal suit, traditional jewelry shimmering softly, bangles chiming with every gentle movement, and intricate mehendi blooming across your hands. Holding your decorated Karwa Chauth Pooja Thali with the channi, diya, and brass lota, you took my breath away.

We gathered on the terrace, staring through the night sky, counting down every passing minute for the moon. And then, glowing red like an ember through the horizon, the moon appeared above the rooftops. Watching you lift the channi to gaze at the moon, and then looking through that very same sieve right into my eyes... the entire world vanished. Tu hi mera chaand hai, Puchki.
