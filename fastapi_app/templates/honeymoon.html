Sentosa & Beachfront Romantic Spots

Trapizza (Siloso Beach, Sentosa)

Wood-fired Pizzas & Pastas by the Sea

Casual-chic Italian right on the sand. Perfect for an afternoon or sunset meal with Pizza Quattro Formaggi, Gnocchi, and refreshing mocktails.

Cassia (Capella Singapore)

Ultra-Luxury Cantonese in Heritage Manor

One of Singapore's most romantic venues. Exceptional vegetarian dim sum (black truffle crystal dumplings) and bespoke menus upon advance request.

Quayside Isle (Sentosa Cove)

Yacht Marina Dining & Mediterranean Delights

Stroll past multi-million dollar yachts. Miska Café (falafels, shakshuka) and Sole Pomodoro (authentic Italian pizzas & risottos).

Carlton Hotel & Marina Bay Spots

CHIJMES Courtyards (Opposite Carlton)

Fairytale Gothic Chapel & Outdoor Dining

Lit by fairy lights under the 19th-century chapel. Great Italian, craft beer, and tapas right across the street from your hotel.

Nalan Vegetarian (Capitol Singapore)

100% Pure Vegetarian Fine Indian Dining

Just 2 minutes walk from Carlton Hotel. High quality North & South Indian dishes, paneer curries, and dosas in clean comfort.

Cultivate Cafe (Maxwell Reserve)

Candlelit Colonial Library Plant-Based Dining

An extraordinary honeymoon ambiance nestled among antique bookshelves. 100% plant-based artisanal pastas, vegan caviart, and gourmet desserts.
