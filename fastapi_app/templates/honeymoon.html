🔌 Universal Adapter & Power Bank Packing Rule Critical

1. Universal Adapter: Singapore uses Type G (UK 3-pin plugs, 230V). Carlton Hotel, Disney Cruise cabins, and Oasia Sentosa require Type G plugs. Pack a universal multi-pin adapter with USB-C GaN ports.
2. Power Banks: STRICTLY FORBIDDEN IN CHECKED LUGGAGE! Must be in your HAND LUGGAGE ONLY (max 20,000mAh per power bank, max 2 per person) per DGCA / Singapore CAAS aviation safety laws.
