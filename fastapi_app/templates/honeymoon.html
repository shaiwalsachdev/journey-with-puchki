Form 14A ⇄ Passport Details Verification Guide

priority_high Must Match Letter-for-Letter

Singapore ICA rejects visa applications if any spelling, date, or number differs from your physical Indian passport bio-data page. Use this pre-flight checklist before handing your signed Form 14A to the agent:

badge Part I: Applicant Particulars
  • • Full Name: Write exactly as shown in the travel document (Given Name + Surname). Do not interchange or add initials.
  • • Date of Birth: Format DD/MM/YYYY matching passport day/month/year exactly.
  • • Place of Birth: State and Country as printed on the passport bio-data page.
  • • Passport No.: Check 8-character alphanumeric number (e.g. 1 letter + 7 digits). Ensure zero 0 vs letter O are accurate.
  • • Issue & Expiry Dates: Verify expiry date is well after June 13, 2027 (> 6 months from arrival).
hotel Part II & III: Stay & Hotel In Singapore
  • • Expected Date of Arrival: 13/12/2026 (Matches DEL ➔ SIN flight ticket).
  • • Purpose of Visit: Select Social ➔ Details: "Tourism / Honeymoon & Disney Cruise".
  • • Where Staying: Select Hotel ➔ Carlton Hotel Singapore (76 Bras Basah Road, Postal Code 189558, Tel: +65 6338 8333).
  • • Local Contact: Fill Hotel Name as Carlton Hotel Singapore (Tourist applicants without relatives list their booked hotel).
check_circle Paste 35x45mm matte photo on Page 1 & Sign Page 4 in blue/black ink.
