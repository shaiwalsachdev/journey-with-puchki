October 10, 2026 • Sandoz Night To My Forever Puchki ❤️

"My dearest Puchki,

Watching you twirl in that ethnic saree, laughing under the picnic sunlight, and running together across city streets today felt like living inside my favorite movie. But the moment you held my hands during the proposal setup, with your sparkling eyes and that shy radiant smile, my heart knew once again that choosing you is the easiest, most beautiful decision I will ever make.

No matter how long the drives were or how tired we got, sitting opposite you at Cafe Sandoz, watching you savor every single bite of that warm Gulab Jamun with the biggest childish smile on your face... that was my true highlight of the entire day.

Counting down these final days till December 5 & 6 is making my heartbeat race in the best way possible. I cannot wait to stand beside you as your husband and love you through every lifetime.

Forever & Always Yours,
Shaiwal
